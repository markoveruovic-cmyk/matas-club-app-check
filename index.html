Matas Club mobile app: analysis of 1,148 user reviews

This report analyses publicly available user reviews of the Matas Club app — 1,000 from Google Play and 148 from the Apple App Store. Overall sentiment is positive and has improved markedly since 2024. The analysis identifies four recurring technical pain points concentrated in authentication, release stability, checkout and payments, and gamification. Each finding is documented with verbatim user quotes, presented in the original Danish alongside English translations.

01Executive summary

Overview of findings

The review sample averages 3.99★ on Google Play and 4.20★ on the App Store, with a clear recovery after a weaker 2021–2023 period. However, 19.7% of Google Play reviews and 16.2% of App Store reviews are 1–2★, and these concentrate around a limited set of recurring, addressable issues.

Strengths

Users consistently mention fast delivery, member offers and discounts, straightforward ordering, and in-store staff. Reviews from the last 12 months average 4.41★ on Google Play and 4.35★ on the App Store.

Primary issues

Login and session management (avg 1.88★ across 41 Google Play mentions), crashes following updates (avg 1.95★), checkout and payment failures including discount miscalculation, and gamification defects (65 mentions — the most frequently raised negative theme).

Release-quality signal

Version-level data shows how individual releases drive sentiment: reviews written on v5.3.3 average 1.90★ and a crash cluster follows v7.4.5 (Oct 2024), while the 7.4.x–7.5.x line otherwise averages above 4.3★. Regressions reaching production are a recurring pattern.

Platform differences

Android reviews skew towards technical failures: login, crashes and payments. Negative iOS reviews most often concern checkout and payments and stock availability.

03Key pain points by platform

Four technical pain points

The pain points below were identified from the 221 negative (1–2★) reviews in the sample. Severity classification reflects mention volume, the average rating of the mentions, recency, and helpful-vote counts. Verbatim evidence for each point follows in Section 04.

PP-01

Login & session management failures

Android — primaryiOSAuthenticationBlocks all other functionality

The lowest-rated theme on Android (avg 1.88★ across 41 mentions). Users report being logged out repeatedly, unexplained password resets, login loops ("sorry we had to interrupt your sign-in"), needing many attempts to sign in, and device-specific failures — while the same credentials work in the browser. The same login loop is reported on iOS, where reinstalling the app does not resolve it. Because authentication gates every other feature, these failures effectively make the app unusable for affected users.

Evidence: 41 GP + 4 AS mentions · recurring from 2024 through May 2026 · quotes Q1–Q6
PP-02

Crashes & instability following updates

Android — primaryiOSStabilityRelease quality

41 Google Play mentions averaging 1.95★. A distinct cluster follows the v7.4.5 release (October–November 2024): the app crashes on launch, locks up, or closes seconds after opening, and reinstalling does not help. iOS users describe the app getting stuck on the start screen. Several reviewers state explicitly that the app worked before the update in question, indicating that regressions are reaching production. The version-level chart in Section 02 (v5.3.3 at 1.90★) shows the same pattern in earlier releases.

Evidence: crash cluster on v7.4.5 (Oct 2024) · quotes Q7–Q10
PP-03

Checkout, payments & discount calculation

AndroidiOSRevenue-criticalKlarna · MobilePay · Anyday

Reviews describe payment methods failing at checkout: Klarna payments that never complete, MobilePay failures, and a default flow perceived as steering users into Anyday financing. In addition, the basket itself is reported to miscalculate multi-buy discounts (3-for-3 offers, two-item bundles) — in one case the missing discount was only discovered on the invoice. Further reports cover gift-card state persisting between orders and subscription items that cannot be combined with regular orders or paid via MobilePay, resulting in split orders and repeated MitID approvals.

Evidence: 35 GP + 12 AS mentions, avg 2.40★ (GP) · quotes Q11–Q17
PP-04

Defects in competitions & gamification

Android — primaryiOSLoyalty & engagementHighest mention volume

Competitions and seasonal games are the most frequently raised negative theme (65 Google Play mentions, avg 2.82★). Reported issues include games freezing mid-play and losing earned points, levels with unwinnable obstacle layouts, accidental taps registered as completed entries, reward catalogues that empty out, perceived imbalance in prize point values, and — because winners are not published — a recurring perception that prizes are never awarded. The engagement layer is currently a measurable source of dissatisfaction with the loyalty programme.

Evidence: 65 GP + 5 AS mentions · Easter/summer game complaints 2025–2026 · quotes Q18–Q22

06Recommendations

Recommended priorities

The following priorities are derived directly from the patterns documented in this report. They are ordered by a combination of severity (average rating of mentions), business impact and recency.

01

Stabilise authentication

Login is the lowest-rated theme in the data (avg 1.88★) and gates all other functionality. The repeated observation that credentials work in the browser but not in the app points to an app-side session or token-lifecycle issue rather than an identity-provider problem, and provides a clear starting point for diagnosis.

02

Strengthen release quality assurance

The crash cluster around v7.4.5 and the 1.90★ average on v5.3.3 indicate that regressions periodically reach production. Pre-release testing across a representative device matrix, staged rollouts and crash-rate release gates would reduce the recurrence of these incidents.

03

Audit the checkout and pricing engine

Failed Klarna and MobilePay payments and miscalculated multi-buy discounts directly affect revenue and trust; in one documented case the discount error was only visible on the invoice. The basket's price calculation and the payment integrations warrant dedicated end-to-end test coverage and production monitoring.

04

Repair or rescope gamification

With 65 mentions averaging 2.82★, the engagement features currently generate measurable dissatisfaction: freezing games, accidental entries and depleted reward catalogues. Fixing the game mechanics, publishing winners and rebalancing the reward economy would address the documented complaints; alternatively, the feature's scope should be reconsidered.

07Data & methodology

How this report was prepared

  • Sources: two scraped exports of public user reviews of the Matas Club app — 1,000 Google Play reviews (18 Oct 2019 – 5 Jun 2026) and 148 Apple App Store reviews (21 Nov 2023 – 4 Jun 2026). No other data sources were used; every statistic and quote in this report comes from these two files.
  • Averages: computed over reviews in the sample (i.e. reviews with written text). They are not the official store-wide star ratings, which also include text-less ratings.
  • Pain-point themes: identified by reading all 221 negative (1–2★) reviews and validated with Danish keyword matching across the full sample (e.g. log ind, lukker ned, betal/Klarna/MobilePay, konkurrence). Keyword counts are indicative; a review can match multiple themes.
  • Quotes: reproduced verbatim, including original spelling and punctuation. English translations were produced for this report; reviews originally written in English are marked as such. Reviewer names are public display names as published on the stores.
  • Version data: app version is available for Google Play reviews only (reviewCreatedVersion); the App Store export did not include version information.
